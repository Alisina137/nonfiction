BUG #1

"This persona isn't finalized yet — click Generate Persona to produce the tone and style breakdown."

Even after Generate Persona has been clicked.

- Strengths appear
- Suggestions appear

- Writing Style

4. The save logic is not recognizing them.
5. The persona finalized validation is incorrect.
