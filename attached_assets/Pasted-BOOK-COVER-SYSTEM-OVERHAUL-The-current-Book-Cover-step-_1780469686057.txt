BOOK COVER SYSTEM OVERHAUL

The current Book Cover step has major UX and quality issues that must be fixed.

PROBLEMS TO SOLVE

1. Cover designs are too generic and look almost identical.
2. Most generated covers are only typography variations.
3. Cover generation does not feel like true AI-powered design.
4. Downloaded files do not match the preview.
5. Export buttons do not produce the expected output.
6. SVG exports contain incorrect dimensions, blank space, or missing elements.
7. Users cannot generate professional-looking Amazon bestseller covers.

}
