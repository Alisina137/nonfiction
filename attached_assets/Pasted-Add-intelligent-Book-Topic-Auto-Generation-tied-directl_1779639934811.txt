Add intelligent "Book Topic Auto-Generation" tied directly to the book title.

GOAL:
When the user selects, generates, or edits a book title, automatically generate a highly relevant and commercially optimized Book Topic field.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT use generic summaries.
The generated topic should sound like a real bestselling nonfiction book concept.

========================
FEATURE REQUIREMENTS
====================

When:

* a suggested title is clicked
  OR
* the book title input changes

Automatically run:
generateBookTopic(title, niche, subNiche, deepNiche)

The AI should analyze:

* emotional wording
* target audience
* transformation promise
* niche psychology
* commercial intent
* self-help market positioning

Then auto-fill the "Book Topic" field instantly.

========================
BOOK TOPIC RULES
================

The generated topic should:

* explain what the book delivers
* mention transformation
* identify the audience
* sound commercially attractive
* feel like Amazon bestseller positioning
* avoid generic descriptions
* stay concise but powerful

Length:
1–3 sentences max.

========================
GOOD EXAMPLES
=============

Title:
"Stoic Calm for Ambitious Men"

Generated Book Topic:
"A practical self-mastery guide helping ambitious men eliminate emotional chaos, build discipline, and develop calm, focused confidence using modern Stoic principles."

---

Title:
"The Discipline Reset for Overthinkers"

Generated Book Topic:
"A tactical mindset system for overthinkers who struggle with inconsistency, distraction, and procrastination, designed to build focus, execution, and emotional control."

---

Title:
"Deep Focus in a Distracted World"

Generated Book Topic:
"A modern productivity framework teaching creators, entrepreneurs, and professionals how to reclaim concentration, eliminate dopamine-driven distractions, and produce meaningful work consistently."
