Improve the manuscript structure and reading flow for both DOCX and PDF exports to make the generated books feel professionally written and properly formatted like premium nonfiction books.

Main Goals:

1. Every chapter must begin on a new page
2. Every chapter should include a short chapter introduction
3. Every section should include a short section introduction
4. Improve readability and content flow
5. Make the book feel naturally structured instead of AI-generated

Requirements:

1. Start Every Chapter on a New Page

Current issue:
Chapters continue directly after the previous one.

Fix:

* Every new chapter must automatically start on a fresh page
* Apply this to both:

  * DOCX
  * PDF

Implementation:

```js id="eymu4r"
insertPageBreakBeforeChapter()
```

Rules:

* Never place two chapters on the same page flow
* Chapter title should appear near top of new page
* Maintain consistent top spacing

2. Add Chapter Introduction Blocks

Before the first section of every chapter, automatically generate a short introductory paragraph.

Purpose:

* Explain what the chapter will cover
* Create smoother reading flow
* Improve professionalism

Example:

```text id="bngzrm"
In this chapter, you will learn how discipline shapes long-term success, why motivation often fails, and how to build systems that create lasting habits.
```

Requirements:

* 1–2 short paragraphs
* concise and engaging
* nonfiction tone
* no repetition
* connected to chapter topic

Placement:

```text id="k5nzhh"
Chapter Title
↓
Chapter Introduction
↓
Section 1
```

3. Add Section Introduction Blocks

Before each section’s main content, generate a brief section overview.

Purpose:

* Introduce the section topic
* Improve transitions
* Make content feel intentionally structured

Example:

```text id="2fh6ea"
This section explores the psychological reasons people struggle with consistency and how identity-based thinking can change behavior.
```

Requirements:

* short and natural
* 2–4 sentences
* should preview the section content
* avoid generic filler text

4. Improve Content Hierarchy

Final structure should look like:

```text id="2rj8x7"
Chapter 1: The Psychology of Discipline

[Chapter Introduction]

1.1 Why Motivation Fails

[Section Introduction]

[Main Content]

1.2 Identity-Based Habits

[Section Introduction]

[Main Content]
```

5. Typography and Spacing

For introductions:

* slightly italicized OR slightly different spacing
* visually separated from main content
* maintain Times New Roman formatting

Spacing:

* chapter intro should have larger spacing below
* section intro should have medium spacing below

6. Prevent AI Repetition

Current AI issue:
Introductions may repeat chapter titles or generic phrases.

Add validation:

* avoid repeated wording
* avoid “In this section/chapter we will discuss...” every time
* vary sentence structure naturally

7. Smart Context Awareness

The introduction generator should use:

* chapter title
* section title
* outline metadata
* previous section context

to generate relevant intros.

8. Export Formatting Rules

DOCX:

* preserve intro styling
* preserve spacing
* keep intros attached to their sections

PDF:

* avoid intro paragraphs splitting awkwardly across pages
* maintain clean spacing

9. Reading Experience Optimization

Goal:
The exported book should read like:

* a professionally edited nonfiction book
* smooth educational content
* natural transitions between topics

NOT:

* isolated AI-generated blocks
* abrupt section jumps
* disconnected chapters

10. Final Quality Goal

The finished exported book should feel:

* intentional
* professionally structured
* smooth to read
* premium-quality
* ready for Amazon KDP publishing
