Refactor the entire “Research” and “Analysis” workflow to eliminate duplication and create a cleaner publishing pipeline.

====================================================
MAIN GOAL
====================================================

The current Research step contains advanced publishing intelligence fields that should actually belong to the Analysis step.

The Analysis step is now becoming the Competitive Intelligence Engine, so anything related to:
- audience psychology
- emotional targeting
- tone analysis
- market intelligence
- publishing strategy
- reader pain points
- writing style detection
- positioning
- bestseller patterns

should be REMOVED from Research and generated automatically inside Analysis.

Research should become lightweight and foundational only.

====================================================
RESEARCH STEP REFACTOR
====================================================

SIMPLIFY the Research step.

Research should ONLY collect core foundational inputs needed before analysis begins.

====================================================
KEEP ONLY THESE IN RESEARCH
====================================================

1. Broad niche
2. Sub-niche
3. Book topic
4. Optional short concept/idea
5. Optional desired transformation
6. Optional custom notes

That’s it.

Research should NOT attempt to do:
- audience profiling
- emotional targeting
- tone strategy
- pain analysis
- positioning
- energy style
- writing fingerprinting
- reader intelligence
- market analysis

because those are now handled automatically by the Analysis engine.

====================================================
REMOVE FROM RESEARCH
====================================================

Remove these sections entirely from the Research step UI:

- Author tone
- Reader preset
- Target audience (specific)
- Core promise
- Reader pain level
- Energy style
- Publishing intelligence text
- Emotional targeting controls
- Writing style controls
- Audience psychology controls
- Any advanced publishing profile section

These fields are now redundant.

====================================================
WHY THIS CHANGE MATTERS
====================================================

Currently the workflow is backwards.

The user is being asked to manually define:
- audience
- tone
- positioning
- emotional style

BEFORE the app has analyzed the market.

Instead:

Research
↓
Analysis (extract intelligence automatically)
↓
Use extracted intelligence throughout generation

This creates:
- smarter automation
- less user friction
- better consistency
- more professional workflow
- cleaner UI
- more accurate outputs

====================================================
NEW RESEARCH STEP PHILOSOPHY
====================================================

Research should feel like:

“Idea Input”

NOT:

“Publishing Strategy Configuration”

The AI should do the strategic heavy lifting automatically after competitor analysis.

====================================================
NEW ANALYSIS RESPONSIBILITIES
====================================================

The Analysis engine should now automatically generate:

- target audience
- emotional triggers
- tone recommendations
- reader pain profile
- transformation promises
- writing style fingerprint
- energy style
- positioning strategy
- market gap analysis
- bestseller DNA
- reader psychology
- chapter structure patterns
- review intelligence

based on competitor books and review mining.

====================================================
AUTO-FILL SYSTEM
====================================================

After Analysis completes:

Auto-fill downstream steps with extracted intelligence.

Examples:

Author Persona:
→ auto-generated from market positioning

Outline:
→ generated from competitor structure intelligence

Writing Style:
→ generated from emotional + audience analysis

Book Description:
→ generated from bestseller copy patterns

Title Suggestions:
→ generated from emotional/title analysis

====================================================
UI/UX IMPROVEMENTS
====================================================

Research step should feel:
- minimal
- clean
- fast
- idea-focused

Analysis step should feel:
- intelligent
- strategic
- data-driven
- powerful

====================================================
EXAMPLE OF IDEAL FLOW
====================================================

STEP 1 — RESEARCH
User enters:
- niche
- sub-niche
- book topic

STEP 2 — ANALYSIS
AI analyzes:
- competitors
- reviews
- audience
- emotions
- positioning
- structure
- market gaps

STEP 3+
Everything else is auto-generated from analysis intelligence.

====================================================
IMPORTANT IMPLEMENTATION RULE
====================================================

Do NOT simply hide old fields.

Actually remove the old logic dependencies from Research and move them into Analysis-generated intelligence.

The Analysis step should become the single source of truth for publishing intelligence.

====================================================
FINAL GOAL
====================================================

The app should feel like a professional AI publishing studio where:

The user provides the IDEA,
and the AI handles the MARKET INTELLIGENCE automatically.
